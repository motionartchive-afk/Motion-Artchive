Policies

Terms of service

Work starts after the one-time package fee is paid. Each package includes the revision rounds and first-draft time shown in Pricing. Changes outside the agreed brief and extra rounds are quoted separately. Delivery times are estimates and depend on brief clarity and feedback speed.

Privacy policy

We collect only what you send us: your name, email, WhatsApp number and project files. We use them to reply and deliver your project, and we do not sell them. Non-essential cookies are used only if you accept them. To access or delete your data, email motionartchive@gmail.com.

Refund policy

You can cancel for a full refund before work starts. After the first draft is delivered, requests are reviewed case by case. If the first draft misses the agreed window, your next revision round is free.

License terms

Every package includes a commercial-use license for the delivered video, for the client named in the order. Files may not be resold or redistributed as templates. Source files, where included, arrive by private link after final payment. Templates are licensed for your own projects only. We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
